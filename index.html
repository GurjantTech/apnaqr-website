FAQ

Questions? We got you.

Is the QR code generator free?

Yes. Every QR type, style, logo option and PNG or SVG download is free, with no sign-up and no watermark.

Is what I type uploaded anywhere?

No. The generator runs entirely in your browser. Your text, Wi-Fi password, UPI ID and logo never leave your device, and we don't store any QR codes.

Do the QR codes expire?

No. These are static QR codes: the content is stored inside the code itself, so they keep working for as long as the link, number or details inside them are valid.

Is the Apna QR app free?

Yes. The scanner, smart actions, history and QR maker are free. There is no subscription and no sign-up.

Does the app upload my data?

No. Codes are read on your phone, and your scan and create history stays in a database on your device. We don't run a server that stores your codes.

Why ads?

Ads keep Apna QR free. You see a small banner, and a short video only when you choose to unlock something. In the EEA and UK you are asked for consent before any personalised ads.

What are premium templates?

A few QR style templates in the app are premium. Watch one short video and they unlock for the rest of the session. No payment needed.

Which barcodes are supported?

QR Code, Data Matrix, Aztec, PDF417, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, Code 128, Code 39, Code 93, Codabar and ITF.

How to scan from gallery?

On the scan screen, tap the gallery button and pick a photo or screenshot. Apna QR reads the code on your phone; the image is never uploaded.

Is UPI scanning safe?

Apna QR only reads the UPI code and opens the UPI app you pick. It never sees your PIN or touches your money. Always check the payee name in your UPI app before paying; Apna QR reminds you every time.
